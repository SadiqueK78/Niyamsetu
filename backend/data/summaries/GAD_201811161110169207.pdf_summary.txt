SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
The resolution aims to consolidate and streamline the allocation of rooms and rates for the new Maharashtra House in New Delhi, enhancing the living conditions for officials residing there.

2. KEY PROVISIONS
- Room allocation and rates for various officials are specified.
- Certain officials are exempt from room charges.
- Additional provisions for special cases and additional room requests are outlined.

3. BENEFICIARIES / TARGET GROUP
Officials residing in the new Maharashtra House in New Delhi, including members of the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly, Vidhan Sabha, and other government servants.

4. FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS
Monetary allocations for room charges and exemptions are specified for various officials.

5. IMPLEMENTATION
The resolution takes effect immediately upon issuance.

Not specified.
